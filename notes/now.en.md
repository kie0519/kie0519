# Now

<sub>Current · Beijing</sub>

I am less interested in producing another agent demo than in helping agents preserve direction, boundaries, and responsibility over longer periods of work.

## Context is not a warehouse

Context is a continuous act of selection. A system must know when to retrieve, when to compact, when to preserve uncertainty, and which information should not survive into the next decision.

A larger window does not guarantee clearer judgment. What matters is showing the model the right thing at the right moment.

## Capability is also boundary

Tools move a model from language into action, while expanding the radius of possible failure.

MCP, Agent Skills, and A2A make capabilities increasingly composable. The harder work moves toward authorization, isolation, recovery, and human intervention. A mature agent system knows not only how to act, but when not to.

## Evals define the product

Evals are not an examination at the end of development. They are business judgment made executable.

If completion, failure, and unacceptable error cannot be defined, the product itself remains undefined. Reliability is not a property supplied by a model alone; it is an outcome maintained by the entire system.

## The interface is becoming action

Agentic coding and computer use are changing the entrance to software. People increasingly express goals, observe execution, and intervene at moments of judgment rather than operating every interface step by step.

The important design problem is not merely a more natural conversation. It is a clearer relationship of responsibility between human and machine.

---

These are not conclusions. They are my present coordinates.
